# Cognitive Flexibility (CogFlex) Lab

*Understanding how minds change*

---

How do we move beyond familiar ways of thinking—and why is changing our minds sometimes so difficult?

The CogFlex lab studies the cognitive processes that allow people to overcome mental blocks, discover new solutions, and reconsider their beliefs. We bring together research on creative problem solving, insight, and metacognition—how we monitor and evaluate our own thinking—to investigate how people interpret information and respond to new evidence.

Our work connects these questions to contemporary challenges, including misinformation, conspiracy beliefs, and socio-cognitive polarization. Through behavioral experiments and studies of beliefs over time, we explore whether the shifts in perspective that help us solve problems can also help us revise our understanding of the world.
